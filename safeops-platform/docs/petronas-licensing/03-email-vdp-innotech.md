# Email 2 — VDP InnoTech interest and submission route

Send this **separately** from the licensing enquiry, and ideally to a different inbox. They are
different questions handled by different people, and combining them invites one answer to the
easier one.

**Before you send:** confirm the current contact route from
[petronas.com/suppliers](https://www.petronas.com/suppliers) rather than reusing an address
from a search result.

---

**Subject:** Expression of interest — Vendor Development Programme (InnoTech), and enquiry on the submission route

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to register interest in the PETRONAS Vendor Development Programme (InnoTech) and
to ask for guidance on the correct submission route for a Malaysian technology product.

**About the company**

I am the founder of a Malaysian business based in Miri, Sarawak, which has developed
**SafeChain**, a workplace safety and health management platform supplied as a subscription
service. It covers incident reporting and investigation, corrective action tracking,
permit-to-work, inspections, asset and equipment records, training and competency, contractor
management, and consolidated safety reporting.

The product is built and running. A demonstration carrying sample data is available at
**safechain.schain.workers.dev**, and I would be glad to arrange a walkthrough.

I should be straightforward about our stage: we are at early customer validation and are
seeking our first pilot deployment. We hold no certifications, have no existing contracts, and
make no claim to prior work with PETRONAS or its contractors.

**My questions**

1. Is VDP InnoTech a pathway separate from PETRONAS vendor licensing and registration, or does
   it require a licence or registration to be held first?
2. What is the current submission route for a technology proposal under InnoTech, and is there
   an open call, a window, or a continuous intake?
3. Does InnoTech apply any eligibility conditions beyond those in the general licensing
   guidelines — in particular any requirement as to company type, Bumiputera ownership, or
   minimum trading history?
4. If my business is not eligible for InnoTech at its present stage, is there another PETRONAS
   technology pathway — for example a technology marketplace or challenge — that would be more
   appropriate?

I have a written proposal prepared and will submit it through whichever route you advise. I
would rather follow the correct process than submit through the wrong one.

Thank you for your time.

## Why it is written this way

It volunteers the weaknesses — no certifications, no contracts, no PETRONAS history — in one
short paragraph, early. An assessor finds those out anyway, and a vendor who states them first
is the one they believe about everything else.

It asks whether InnoTech sits behind licensing rather than assuming either answer, because
that is the question your whole strategy turns on and nothing published settles it.

It offers a live demonstration, which almost no early-stage applicant can do, and it does not
ask for money.
