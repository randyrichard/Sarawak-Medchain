# Email 1 — SWEC category and Sarawak eligibility enquiry

Send this before spending anything. It asks the two questions that decide your route.

**Send to:** the Licensing and Registration Section via the contact channel on
[petronas.com/suppliers/licensing-procurement-malaysia](https://www.petronas.com/suppliers/licensing-procurement-malaysia),
or raise it through [PLMS](https://licensing.petronas.com/) if you can open an account without
a completed application. Confirm the current address before sending — do not use one from memory.

**Before you send:** fill the two bracketed items, and attach nothing. A first enquiry with
attachments reads as an application; this is a question.

---

**Subject:** Enquiry — applicable SWEC category for workplace safety management software, and eligibility of a Sarawak-registered sole proprietorship

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to seek guidance on two points before preparing an application for PETRONAS
licensing or registration. I would rather ask than submit an application on an assumption.

**1. Applicable SWEC category**

My business supplies a workplace safety and health management software platform, provided to
customers as a subscription service hosted online. It covers incident reporting and
investigation, corrective action tracking, permit-to-work, inspections, training and
competency records, contractor management, and safety reporting. It is a software product
supplied as a service; it is not consultancy, not manpower supply, and not hardware.

Having reviewed the Updated SWEC List of 29 August 2022, the categories that appear closest are:

- **13101600S** — Application Software / Software License Leasing
- **13101400S** — Application Software / Software Development Services

Neither description corresponds exactly to a subscription software service of this kind. I
would be grateful if you could confirm:

a. Which SWEC category applies to software supplied on a subscription basis for workplace
   safety management;
b. Whether the 29 August 2022 SWEC list remains current, or has been superseded; and
c. Whether any Minimum Technical Requirements apply to the category you identify.

**2. Eligibility of a Sarawak-registered sole proprietorship**

My business is a sole proprietorship registered in Miri, Sarawak, under the Sarawak Business
Names Register (registration number [ MRI/BNR/718/2026 ]).

The General Guidelines v15.0 state that Registration of Business (ROB) applicants must be
registered with SSM as a sole proprietorship or partnership, and that "ROB is only applicable
for West Malaysia". I would be grateful for your confirmation of:

a. Whether a sole proprietorship registered in Sarawak under the state Business Names
   Register may apply for PETRONAS licensing or registration;
b. If not, whether any route exists for a Sarawak-based sole proprietorship short of
   incorporating a Sdn Bhd; and
c. If incorporation is required, whether a newly incorporated company with no trading history
   can satisfy the positive net worth requirement, and what financial evidence would be
   expected in place of audited accounts.

I am at an early stage and would prefer to establish the correct route before incurring cost
or restructuring the business. Any guidance you are able to give would be much appreciated.

Yours faithfully,

**Randy Richard**
Founder

Miri, Sarawak

## Why it is written this way

It asks, rather than asserts. It quotes the guideline that creates the problem, so the officer
answering does not have to look it up. It names the two SWEC codes you have already found,
which shows you have read the list and makes it easy to confirm or correct you. And it states
plainly that you would rather ask than spend — officers deal with applicants who did the
opposite all day.

It claims nothing about the product that is not true, and it does not ask for a favour.
