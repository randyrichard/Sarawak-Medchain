## 8. Backups, and what they do not cover

We take a nightly database backup and a copy of uploaded files, and keep them off the
server they protect.

**Read this part.** A backup is a recovery mechanism, not a guarantee against loss. Up to
**[PERIOD — currently 24 hours]** of work could be lost in a total failure. Recovery time is
a target and not a commitment, and has not yet been measured on a live deployment.

The in-product restore point is an undo button for a bad import — it does not capture
incident timelines, permit precaution checklists, gas tests, isolations, signatures or
attachments, and it is stored inside the database it protects. It is not disaster recovery.
The product says so where the button is.

**Keep your own export.** You can produce one in a click, and it is the only copy entirely
within your control.
